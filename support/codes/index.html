Before sharing a diagnostic report

Review the report and remove anything you do not want to send. Most listed codes use privacy-safe technical context. OFS-1904 and OFS-9001 may include exception details and require your consent before sharing.

For additional help, contact info@offisen.app and include the code, correlation ID, timestamp, Offisen version, and a short description of what happened.
